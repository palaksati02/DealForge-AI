A2A Protocol & Interop

Agent-to-Agent Protocol: CrewAI orchestrator dispatches an HTTP REST/JSON-RPC A2AMessageEnvelope to independent Google ADK Risk Agent microservice at port 8001. Implements correlation IDs, retry backoff, and local ADK fallback.
